# TechNova — 20-Screen Interactive Mobile App Wireframing Prototype

> **" Technology Simplified\** — Complete high-fidelity mobile application wireframe and interactive prototype engineered for modern consumer technology ecosystems.

---

## 🌟 Overview

TechNova is an end-to-end interactive mobile app prototype featuring **20 connected screens**, complete state management (Cart, Wishlist, Orders, Notifications), realistic touch ergonomics, dark cyber tech aesthetics, and complete user flows without dead-end routes.

---

## 📱 The 20 Screen Architecture

1. **Splash Screen** — Animated branding, tagline, and instant \Get Started\ touch launch.
2. **Onboarding (3 Slides)** — Interactive carousel (Hardware Discovery, Express Delivery, Official TechCare Warranty) with swipe and dot navigation.
3. **Login / Register** — Account authentication, password recovery, Google/Apple social sign-in.
4. **Home Screen ⭐** — Dark cyber tech dashboard, VIP greeting, interactive search bar, promotional banner, 4 category chips, featured flagships, and 5-tab bottom navigation.
5. **Categories** — 6 hardware categories (Laptops, Phones, Audio, Gaming Gear, Wearables, IoT).
6. **Category Products** — Filtered catalog with sorting, product specs, and 1-tap cart additions.
7. **Search Screen** — Voice mic simulation, recent searches, trending tags.
8. **Search Results** — Live keyword results with active filter badges.
9. **Product Details ⭐** — Interactive hardware hero gallery, 3 angle previews, technical specs matrix, and 1-tap \Add to Cart\ / \Buy Now\.
10. **Wishlist** — Saved favorites with real-time stock badges and 1-tap move to cart.
11. **Cart Screen ⭐** — Dynamic quantity counters, item removal, discount coupon engine (TECH2026), and checkout breakdown.
12. **Checkout** — Shipping address selector, delivery speed options, order summary.
13. **Payment Screen** — UPI (GPay/PhonePe), Cards, Net Banking, and COD with 256-bit SSL encryption badge.
14. **Order Success** — Transaction confirmed banner, generated order ID, delivery estimate, and live tracking trigger.
15. **My Orders** — Filter tabs (Active, Delivered, Cancelled) with order status badges.
16. **Order Details & Live Tracking** — 5-stage vertical timeline tracking with BlueDart courier ID.
17. **Profile Account Hub** — Titan VIP member hero, 4 quick stat tiles, live shipment tracker card, and account settings.
18. **Settings** — Preferences, notifications, security, and theme controls.
19. **Notifications Feed** — Timestamped delivery alerts, coupons, and wishlist price drops.
20. **Help & Support Desk** — 24/7 live chat trigger, FAQ topics, and toll-free helpline.

## 🎨 Key Features & Technical Highlights

- **Dark Cyber Tech Theme Default**: Deep midnight obsidian palette (\#0B1120\), electric cyan/blue neon accents.
- **100% Touchable 1-Tap Navigation**: Every card, button, banner, and bottom nav icon transitions seamlessly.
- **Zero Awkward Scrolling on App Flow**: Splash, Onboarding, Login, and Order Success fit 100% of the viewport.
- **Automated Verification**: Includes automated regression suite (\ est_all_flows.js\) validating all 20 screens and continuous routes.
