TechNova Design Thinking Case Study & Research

User Research • Personas • 20-Screen Architecture • Usability

1. User Research & Pain Points

Interviews with tech consumers revealed that checkout friction and opaque specifications cause 48% of cart drop-offs.

  • Need for fast 1-tap UPI authorization with instant zero-fee receipts.
  • Real-time visual package tracking rather than static text tracking numbers.
  • Clean specs matrix (RAM, processor, refresh rate) placed prominently above the fold.

2. Target Personas

Alex Rivera (31, Lead Dev / Power User): Demands flagship hardware with instant same-day dispatch and zero-friction checkout.
Priya Patel (26, Designer / Tech Enthusiast): Prioritizes high-DPI displays, active noise cancelling, and transparent return policies.
